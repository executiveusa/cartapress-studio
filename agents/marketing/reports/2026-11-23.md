## Original organic content DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED

**A restaurant's specials should not force a full menu redesign.**

For cafés, bars and restaurants with rotating features, keep the main menu stable and put short-lived offerings on a clearly dated insert. Make the dish name, price, service window and dietary notes easy to find. Check every detail against the owner's approved source before printing.
