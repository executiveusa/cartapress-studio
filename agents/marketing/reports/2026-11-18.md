## Organic content DRAFT — not published

**Headline: One restaurant, several menus. One consistent identity.**

Breakfast, dinner, a cocktail tasting and happy hour may each need a different layout, but they should still feel like the same restaurant. A good menu system separates the parts that change often from the parts that stay constant: typography, colors, price hierarchy, dietary symbols and brand details. Use an editable dated insert for seasonal drinks, and verify every price against the owner's current source before printing.
