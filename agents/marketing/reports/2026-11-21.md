## Original organic content DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED

**Headline: Your ordering menu and your table menu can do different jobs.**

An online ordering catalog may need every modifier, pickup option and category. A printed menu needs a hierarchy that guests can scan at the table: dish name, short description, price, and only the choices that matter. For frequently changing specials, use a dated insert rather than redesigning every page. Always proof names, prices, allergens and service hours against the restaurant owner's approved source.

CartaPress Studio creates original menu designs from client-approved content. Refresh starts at $79 for up to 25 dishes. Printing and printer-specific prepress are separate. Publication remains on hold pending verified live destination and approval.
