## Organic content DRAFT — not published

**Headline: A seasonal menu should be easy to change without losing its character.**

A restaurant can keep its visual identity consistent while changing a weekly wine list, lunch sandwich, or seasonal side. The trick is to design a system: one approved source for names and prices, a clear hierarchy for each service, an editable insert for rotating items, and a dated proof the owner signs off on before printing. Don't assume a PDF found online is the current menu—verify the version first.
